The Red Brigade is a team of trained volunteers who respond to fires and emergencies in our community, day and night.

Emergency? Call 911 right away.

What We Do

  • Fire Response: Fast action to put out structure, brush, and vehicle fires.
  • Rescue: Helping people who are trapped or in danger.
  • Medical Aid: First aid until medical help arrives.
  • Safety Education: Free talks for schools, homes, and businesses.

Join the Brigade

You do not need experience. We provide the training and the gear. All you need is a willingness to help.

  1. Fill in the form below.
  2. Meet our team at an open training night.
  3. Complete basic firefighter training.
  4. Start serving your community.

Fire Safety Tips

  • Test your smoke alarms every month.
  • Plan two ways out of every room.
  • Never leave cooking unattended.
  • Keep a fire extinguisher in the kitchen.
  • Unplug devices that overheat.
